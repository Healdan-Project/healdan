---
title: Document Permissions
weight: 5
---

A document normally follows its folder's permissions: whoever can see the folder can see the document.

## Sharing One Document More Widely

A document can also be given to someone who can't see its folder, for a one-off or for a while. They can't find it
by browsing, because they can't open the folder, but they can reach it by its doc # or a link. From there they can
open it, preview it, download it, and see its properties and versions, as their permissions on the document allow.

To change a document's permissions, select it and open its **Security** tab. The tab appears only if you have the
Security permission on that document.

## Folders Work Differently

A folder can be more restricted than the folder above it, but never less. So a document can be shared beyond its
folder, but a folder can't open up a matter to people the matter's workspace doesn't let in. See
[Stores and Matter Workspaces](../stores-and-matter-workspaces/#permissions).
