Healdan's web page has a top bar across the top, a left pane down the left side and the main area beside it. The
colours follow your browser's light or dark setting.

## The Top Bar

- **Healdan's mark and name** on the left take you back to the start page.
- **Help** opens the User Guide, and the notices for the open-source software Healdan uses, each in a new window.
- **Your menu**, under your initials, shows your name and has Classic Interface and Sign Out. Classic Interface opens
  the older web page, on the same sign-in. Sign Out ends your session.

## The Left Pane

The left pane scrolls on its own when it's longer than the window. From the top it has:

- **Recent:** **Documents** is your recent documents, newest first, and is the page you start on.
- **Recent Matters:** the matters you've worked in lately, each with its client beside it in smaller, paler type.
- **My Matters:** the matters you've chosen to keep at hand, as a tree. Open a matter to see its folders and search
  folders, and open a folder to see its own folders. A search folder has its own icon. The tree opens to the folder
  you're in, and **+** beside the heading adds the matter you're in.
- **My Favorites:** documents and folders you've added as favorites.

[My Matters and Recent Matters](../../finding-documents/my-matters-and-recent-matters/) explains the first two.
If your firm calls matters something else, such as projects, the headings use its word: Recent Projects and My
Projects.

## The Main Area

When you sign in, the main area shows **Recent Documents**: the documents you filed, changed, checked in or out, or
downloaded lately, newest first. Click one to select it. Its doc # goes into the page's address, so the link you
copy takes someone to the same place, and Back and Reload keep it.

Open a matter or a folder from the left pane, and the main area lists what's in it: see [Opening a
Matter](../../working-with-matters/stores-and-matter-workspaces/#opening-a-matter).

## The Keyboard

In the left pane's tree, the up and down arrow keys move between rows, the right arrow opens a matter or folder, the
left arrow closes it, and Enter opens the row. In a folder's list, the up and down arrow keys move the selection, and
Enter opens a folder. Esc closes a menu.

## When Something Goes Wrong

- **"You've been signed out."** Your session lapsed, or you signed out in another tab. **Sign In** brings you back
  to the page you were on.
- **"Not found, or you can't see it."** The page, folder or document doesn't exist, or you don't have permission to
  read it. Healdan says the same for both.
- **"Files are unavailable for now. Try again in a few minutes."** Healdan can't reach where your files are kept.
  Your work isn't lost. Ask your administrator if it lasts.
- **"Something went wrong."** Try Again asks once more. If it keeps failing, tell your administrator.
