The Classic Interface is Healdan's first web page, which is LogicalDOC's screens with Healdan's changes: its name and
logo, the doc #, matters and the search box. Healdan's newer web app, at `/app/`, is where signing in takes you.
The app doesn't have everything yet, so the Classic Interface is still there for what it lacks.

## What's Still There

Everything the app doesn't do yet is in the Classic Interface, such as the screens for notes, tags, sharing a
document, locking, permissions and your messages.

## Getting There and Back

- **To the Classic Interface:** in the app, open your menu, under your initials in the top bar, and choose
  **Classic Interface**.
- **Back to the app:** in the Classic Interface, choose **Open in Healdan** in the top bar, beside the support
  button.

You don't sign in again either way. Both pages use the one sign-in, and signing out of either signs you out of
both.

## If Signing In Takes You Here

Your administrator can set Healdan to open the Classic Interface after signing in, with **Start In** in
[Healdan Settings](../../admin/healdan-settings/). **Open in Healdan** then takes you to the app whenever you want it.
