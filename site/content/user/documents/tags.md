Tags are short words you put on documents to group them across matters and folders: `lease` on every lease,
`completion` on what a completion needs. A document can have several, and anyone who can read it sees them.

## Adding and Removing Tags

A document's tags are on its **Properties** tab, beside **Tags**.

1. Choose the pencil beside the tags.
2. To add one, type it in **New tag** and press Enter, or pick a tag already in use from the **Tags** list.
3. To remove one, choose the cross on it.
4. Choose **Save** in the bar above the tabs, or **Cancel** to leave the tags as they were.

You need the Write permission on the document. Changing its tags makes its next snapshot, and is recorded in its
[history](../document-history/) as "Document metadata have been changed".

## Finding Documents by Tag

- **On the Search tab,** open the **Tags** section. Choose a letter, or type in **Other character**, to list the tags
  that start with it, with how many documents have each. Choose a tag to list its documents. **Search in current
  hits** narrows the documents already listed instead.
- **In a full-text search,** the **Search tab's Full-text** section looks in tags as well as file names and contents.

## The Tags Dashboard

The Dashboard's **Tags** tab shows the tags in use:

- **Most used tags,** with how many documents have each;
- **Tags,** by first letter, as on the Search tab;
- **Tag Cloud,** each tag sized by how many documents have it.

Choose a tag in any of them to list its documents on the Search tab.

The lists of tags here and on the Search tab, and their counts, are brought up to date every half hour, so a new tag
can take that long to appear in them.
