Healdan doesn't use aliases. Some menus still offer them, because they come from LogicalDOC, but Healdan refuses
them everywhere a document can be.

## What LogicalDOC Means by an Alias

- **A document alias** is a shortcut: an entry in one folder that opens a document kept in another.
  **Paste as alias** makes one, from a document you've copied.
- **A folder alias** is a folder that shows another folder's contents. **Create alias** makes one.

## Why Healdan Refuses Them

Every document Healdan holds is in a matter, and every matter is in a Store. Inside a Store, Healdan refuses an
alias of either kind, with "Shortcuts aren't allowed inside Stores." Nothing is created.

- **A document has one doc #.** A shortcut can't carry a number of its own, so it would be a second entry with no
  number, or with the original's.
- **A document belongs to one matter.** Its client, matter and the rest of its properties come from the folder
  it's filed in, and so do its permissions. A shortcut in another matter would show it there with the wrong
  properties, to people that matter's permissions don't cover.

## What to Use Instead

- **To connect two documents,** such as a letter and the reply to it, link them. See
  [Links Between Documents](../links-between-documents/).
- **To keep a document or folder you use often close at hand,** bookmark it. See [Bookmarks](../bookmarks/).
- **To have a document in two matters,** copy it into the other matter. The copy is a new document with its own
  doc #. See [Copying, Moving and Deleting](../copying-moving-and-deleting/).
