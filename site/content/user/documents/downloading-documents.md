Downloading saves a copy of a document's file on your computer. The document in Healdan stays as it was, and nothing
you do to your copy reaches it. To change a document, check it out, or open it in LibreOffice with the
[Healdan Helper](../../office/).

You need the Download permission on a document to download it. Without it, **Download** is greyed out.

## One Document

Right-click the document and choose **Download**. The download button on the toolbar above the list does the same,
and so does the **Download** link beside **Permalink** on the document's **Properties** tab.

## Several Documents

Select them, then right-click one and choose **Download**, or use the toolbar's download button. They come as one
zip file, named after their folder, such as `export-Correspondence.zip`. A document you can't download is left out
of it.

**Download package**, on the [Links](../links-between-documents/) tab's right-click menu, zips a document with the
documents linked to it.

## A Whole Folder

Right-click the folder and choose **Export to ZIP**. It's greyed out unless you have both the Export and the Download
permission on the folder: ask your administrator if you need them.

## An Earlier Version

Open the document's **Versions** tab, right-click the version and choose **Download**, or use the **Download** link
in its **Permalink** column. See [Versions](../versions/).

## A Password-Protected Document

Healdan asks for the document's password, "The document is protected by password:", before it shows you anything of
the document, and remembers it until you close or reload the page. See
[Locking a Document](../locking-a-document/#password-protection).

Leave password-protected documents out when you download several at once. With one among them, Healdan says "Some
documents are protected by password", and the zip doesn't hold everything you selected. Download each of those on
its own.

## What Healdan Records

Each download is in the document's history, as "The document has been downloaded". See
[Document History](../document-history/).

## Dropbox

Healdan doesn't connect to Dropbox. To put a document there, download it, then upload it to Dropbox yourself, if
your firm allows it.
