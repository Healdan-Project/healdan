A bookmark keeps a document or folder you use often a click away. Your bookmarks are yours alone: nobody else sees
them.

## Adding a Bookmark

Right-click a document or a folder and choose **Add bookmark**. Bookmarking the same one twice adds nothing.

A bookmark on a matter's workspace is one of your [My Matters](../../finding-documents/my-matters-and-recent-matters/).
**Add to My Matters** makes the same bookmark.

## The Bookmarks Section

The **Bookmarks** section of the Documents screen's left pane lists your bookmarks. Click one to go to it: a folder
opens in the folder tree, and a document is selected in its folder. Right-click one for:

- **Edit,** to change the bookmark's name or description. The document or folder keeps its own name;
- **Download,** for a document;
- **Delete,** which deletes the bookmark, never the document or folder;
- **Open in Folder.**

A bookmark on a document or folder that's been deleted isn't listed.

## Favorites in LibreOffice

Your bookmarks are the **Favorites** of the [Healdan Helper](../../office/)'s windows, apart from your matters, which
are its My Matters:

- **Save to Healdan** lists your favourite folders, to file into;
- **Open from Healdan** lists your favourite folders and documents.

They leave out anything you can no longer read. The star in those windows adds a folder or document to your
favourites, or takes it off, and the web page's Bookmarks section shows the change. See
[Saving to Healdan](../../office/saving-to-healdan/#favourites).
