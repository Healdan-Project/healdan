Backup and Restore Procedure

Version 1 – 15 October 2026 – owner Sam Okafor, IT and Office Manager – approved by Helen Ashcombe, Partner

1.   What we back up

What

Where it lives

Healdan: its database and its document store

The server, healdan.ashcombe-reeve.example

The accounts and time-recording system

The office server, office.ashcombe-reeve.example

The email archive (an export of every mailbox)

The office server, office.ashcombe-reeve.example

2.   When and where

2.1   Nightly at 23:00, a copy of everything in step 1 goes to the office network storage, nas.ashcombe-reeve.example. We keep 30 nightly copies.

2.2   Weekly, early on Sunday, the newest nightly copy is encrypted and sent to Marlvault Backup Ltd. We keep 12 weekly copies.

2.3   Monthly, on the first Saturday, a copy is written to an encrypted drive that is kept off site. We keep 12 monthly copies.

3.   Who does what

3.1   Sam Okafor runs the backups. He reads the backup report that arrives by email each morning, and if a nightly copy has failed he fixes it that day. If two nights in a row fail he tells Helen Ashcombe. Tom Reeve stands in when Sam is away.

3.2   Helen Ashcombe keeps the off-site drive. The key that opens the encrypted copies is in a sealed envelope in the strongroom, and Helen holds a second copy.

3.3   Everyone else keeps client documents in Healdan only, never on a personal device, and tells Sam at once if a file goes missing.

4.   Testing a restore

4.1   Every quarter, on a Saturday, Sam restores the latest nightly copy of Healdan onto a spare machine, checks that the database is complete and that a sample of documents opens, and writes the result in the backup log.

4.2   The last test was on Saturday 25 July 2026. The next is on Saturday 24 October 2026.

5.   If a file is lost

5.1   Tell Sam the matter, the name of the file and when you last saw it.

5.2   Sam first looks in Healdan for the deleted document or an earlier version, which puts most losses right in a few minutes.

5.3   If it is not there, Sam restores the document from the newest nightly copy that has it, into a safe place, and checks that it opens.

5.4   Sam puts the document back in its matter, tells you, and notes what he did in the backup log.

6.   If the server fails

6.1   Tell Sam at once. Sam tells Helen, and rings Marlbridge Computer Services Ltd, whose warranty covers the server, on 01632 960610.

6.2   Until the server is back, keep working from email and your notes, and do not save client documents anywhere else.

6.3   When the server is repaired or replaced, Sam restores Healdan from the newest nightly copy and checks the restored matters before anyone logs in. At most the work since 23:00 the night before is lost.

6.4   If the office network storage has failed too, Sam restores from Marlvault’s newest weekly copy, which can be up to a week old. Staff will then redo the lost work from email and notes. Marlvault’s number is 01632 960622.
