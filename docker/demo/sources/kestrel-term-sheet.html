Term Sheet

Series A investment in Kestrel Software Ltd – 18 September 2026

Non-binding, except the paragraphs on confidentiality, exclusivity and costs

This term sheet sets out the terms on which Merrowdown Ventures LLP, as lead investor, and Pellridge Capital Partners LP are ready to invest in Kestrel Software Ltd. Apart from paragraphs 18 to 20, which bind everyone who signs it, it states what we intend and nobody is bound by it. We are not bound to invest until the subscription agreement has been signed.

1. The Company

Kestrel Software Ltd, Kingfisher Yard, 22 Ropewalk, Marlbridge WX6 1KS (company number to be inserted), which develops and sells Kestrel Rota, a staff-scheduling service for care homes and clinics.

2. The Founders

Ines Kowalczyk, Chief Executive, and Tobias Wren, Chief Technology Officer.

3. The Investors

Merrowdown Ventures LLP (the Lead Investor) and Pellridge Capital Partners LP.

4. The investment

£4,000,000 for 2,500,000 Series A preferred shares: Merrowdown Ventures LLP £3,000,000 (1,875,000 shares) and Pellridge Capital Partners LP £1,000,000 (625,000 shares).

5. Price and valuation

£1.60 a share. The pre-money valuation is £16,000,000, fully diluted: 10,000,000 shares, counting the 1,000,000 shares in the Company’s option pool as if they were already issued. After the investment there will be 12,500,000 shares (£20,000,000 post-money) and the Investors will together hold 20% of them.

6. Use of the money

To grow the engineering and customer-support teams and to build a mobile app for Kestrel Rota, under a plan the board will agree.

7. Liquidation preference

On a sale or winding up, the Series A shares are paid first, up to 1x their subscription price (1x, non-participating). They do not then share in what is left, unless converting to ordinary shares first would give them more.

8. Conversion

Each Series A share converts into one ordinary share when its holder chooses, and automatically on a stock market listing.

9. Anti-dilution

Broad-based weighted-average protection if the Company later issues shares at less than £1.60 a share.

10. The board

Three directors: the two Founders and one director appointed by the Lead Investor. Felix Amberley will be the first.

11. Matters needing investor consent

The Company will need the Lead Investor’s written consent to: change its articles or the rights of any shares; issue shares or options other than from the option pool; sell the business or its intellectual property; borrow more than £250,000; pay a dividend; or change the nature of its business.

12. Information

Annual accounts within 120 days after the year end, management accounts each quarter, and the budget for each year before it starts.

13. Warranties

The Company and the Founders will give the usual warranties about the business, its accounts, its customers, its software and its people. The limits on claims will be negotiated in the subscription agreement.

14. The Founders

Each Founder will work for the Company full time and, after leaving, will not compete with it for 12 months.

15. Conditions

Due diligence that satisfies the Investors on the Company’s finances, contracts, people and law, and on who owns its software; agreed documents; approval by each Investor’s investment committee; and no material change in the business before completion.

16. Documents and timetable

The Company’s solicitors, Ashcombe Reeve LLP, will prepare the first drafts of the subscription agreement and the new articles. The Investors’ solicitors are Brindley Moss LLP. We aim to complete on 23 October 2026.

17. Governing law

English law, and the courts of England.

18. Confidentiality
(binding)

Each party will keep this term sheet, and everything it learns about the others in the course of the investment, confidential, and will use it only for the investment. This does not stop a party telling its professional advisers, or disclosing what the law requires.

19. Exclusivity
(binding)

Until 2 November 2026 the Company and the Founders will not seek or accept an investment in the Company, or an offer for it or its business, from anyone else, and will tell the Lead Investor at once of any approach.

20. Costs
(binding)

The Company will pay the Investors’ reasonable legal costs of the investment, up to £25,000 plus VAT, when it completes. Otherwise each party pays its own costs.
