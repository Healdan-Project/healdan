Kestrel Rota – customer licence terms, version 3.2 – our review

I have reviewed Kestrel Rota’s customer licence terms, version 3.2 of January 2025, which you sent us on 29 September. They are short and fair to customers. I recommend five changes: four to protect Kestrel, and one about the desktop app.

1.  Liability for a data breach (clause 9.3). Clause 9.1 caps your liability at the fees paid in the last 12 months, but clause 9.3 leaves liability for a data breach unlimited. One incident involving a care home’s staff records could cost far more than that customer’s fees, and insurers will not cover a risk with no ceiling. I recommend capping it at three times the yearly fees.

2.  Renewal by public-sector customers (clause 4.2). A renewal for 12 months unless 30 days’ notice is given is usual, but public bodies often cannot stop a renewal in 30 days, because their decisions go through committees and budget rounds. I suggest they get 90 days to give notice, and that you remind them 120 days before renewal.

3.  Service credits (clause 5.4). Credits are not capped, so a long outage could cost you more than the month’s fees. I recommend capping the total credits for a month at 30% of that month’s share of the fees.

4.  Anonymised data (clause 6). The terms say nothing about using data to improve Kestrel Rota, so today you may not. I suggest a right to use anonymised data, from which no person or customer can be identified, to improve the service.

5.  The desktop app and open source. Tobias’s list of open-source components shows that the desktop app bundles a component under a copyleft licence. If you distribute the app, such a licence can oblige you to make your own source code available. The component must be replaced, or its licence terms met, before the app is next distributed to anyone, and it is being disclosed to the investors. I would hold back new copies until this is settled.

The changes would apply to each customer at its renewal, on 60 days’ notice under clause 10.2. If you agree, I will prepare version 3.3.
