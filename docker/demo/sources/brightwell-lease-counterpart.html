1.    Definitions and interpretation

1.1    In this lease these words have the meanings given to them here.

Plan means the plan annexed to this lease, drawing WL/04/01 revision A.

Premises means the property described in the Particulars, including any part of it.

Rent means the yearly rent reserved by clause 3, as reviewed under clause 3.3.

Term means the period of ten years beginning on the Term Start Date.

Term Start Date means 1 November 2026.

VAT means value added tax, and any tax that replaces it.

1.2    A reference to a clause is to a clause of this lease. Headings are for convenience and do not affect what the lease means. Where the Tenant is more than one person, its obligations are joint and several.

2.    Grant

2.1    The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for the Term, with the rights in clause 2.2 and subject to the rights in clause 2.3, the Tenant paying the Rent and performing its obligations in this lease.

2.2    The Tenant may, in common with the Landlord and others, use the estate road to reach the Premises, may park two private vehicles in the spaces numbered 7 and 8 and shaded on the Plan, may keep its refuse bins in the bin store shown on the Plan beside the east side of the Premises, and may display a sign for its business at the entrance to the estate.

2.3    The Landlord may, on reasonable notice except in an emergency, enter the Premises to inspect them, to repair neighbouring property, and to run services through them, causing as little disturbance as it can.

3.    Rent

3.1    The Tenant shall pay the Landlord a yearly rent of £45,000 plus VAT, in equal quarterly instalments in advance on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October. The first instalment is due on the Term Start Date, for the period up to the next quarter day.

3.2    No Rent is payable for the first three months of the Term.

3.3    The Rent shall be reviewed on the fifth anniversary of the Term Start Date. From that date the Rent shall be the open market rent of the Premises at that date, but no more than ten per cent above the Rent then payable and never less than the Rent then payable. If the parties have not agreed the new Rent within three months, either of them may ask an independent surveyor, agreed between them, to decide it.

3.4    If any Rent is unpaid fourteen days after it falls due, the Tenant shall pay interest on it from the due date until payment, at three per cent a year above the base rate of the Landlord’s bank.

4.    The Tenant’s obligations

4.1    The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good repair and condition, except for fair wear and tear and damage by an insured risk, and shall not let them fall into disrepair. The Tenant is not required to put the Premises into any better repair or condition than that shown in the schedule of condition in the Schedule.

4.2    The Tenant shall use the Premises only for baking, and for the retail sale of bread and confectionery, and for serving food and drink to customers for consumption on the Premises. The Tenant shall obtain, at its own cost, any planning permission that use needs before it begins.

4.3    The Tenant shall not make structural alterations to the Premises. It may make other alterations with the Landlord’s written consent, which the Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold or delay.

4.4    The Tenant shall pay the Landlord a fair share of the premium for insuring the Premises, within 14 days of being asked, and shall give the Landlord a copy of the insurance schedule if it asks for one.

4.5    The Tenant shall pay the Landlord’s reasonable and proper costs of an application for consent under this lease that the Landlord grants.

4.6    The Tenant shall pay the Landlord 8.5 per cent of the costs the Landlord reasonably incurs in running the estate of which the Premises form part, including the repair and lighting of the estate road, landscaping, security, the insurance of the common parts, a reserve for future repairs, and a management fee (the service charge). The Landlord shall send the Tenant its budget for each year, and the Tenant shall pay its share in four equal quarterly instalments on the days the Rent is payable, with any difference put right when the year’s accounts are known.

4.7    On the Term Start Date the Tenant shall pay the Landlord a deposit of £13,500, being one quarter’s Rent with VAT. The Landlord shall hold the deposit until 1 November 2029 and then return it to the Tenant if no Rent is owing. If Rent is owing then, the Landlord may use the deposit to pay it and shall return only what is left.

5.    The Landlord’s obligations

5.1    The Landlord shall allow the Tenant to enjoy the Premises during the Term without interruption by the Landlord or anyone claiming through it.

5.2    The Landlord shall keep the Premises insured against fire, flood and the other usual risks for their full rebuilding cost, and shall keep the estate road in reasonable repair.

6.    Ending the lease

6.1    The Landlord may end this lease by re-entering the Premises if any Rent is unpaid 21 days after it falls due, or if the Tenant breaks any other obligation and does not put it right within a reasonable time after the Landlord has given notice.

6.2    The Tenant may end this lease on the fifth anniversary of the Term Start Date by giving the Landlord at least six months’ written notice, if it gives the Landlord vacant possession of the Premises on that date, and has paid the Rent and has carried out its obligations in this lease up to that date.

7.    Notices

7.1    A notice under this lease must be in writing, and delivered by hand or sent by pre-paid first-class post to the other party at its address at the head of this lease, or at any other address in England or Wales that it has told the other party about.

8.    Governing law

8.1    This lease, and any dispute arising from it, is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
