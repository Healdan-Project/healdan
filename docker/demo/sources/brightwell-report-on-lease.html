Unit 4, Weir Lane Trading Estate – report on the lease

The landlord, Penwick Property Limited, has now accepted the changes we asked for, on the conditions I describe below. Your email of 1 October about the plan, the break and the deposit has been taken into account. This letter sets out the terms as they now stand, so that you can approve them before we prepare the lease for signing.

Term

Ten years from 1 November 2026.

Rent

£45,000 a year plus VAT, from 1 November 2026, paid quarterly in advance. You pay nothing for the first three months, so nothing is payable until 31 January 2027.

Rent review

On 1 November 2031, to the open market rent. The new rent can be at most 10% above the rent then payable, and it can never be less than the rent then payable.

Break

You may end the lease on 1 November 2031 by giving at least six months’ notice, if you hand back the unit empty and have paid the rent.

Repairs

You keep the unit in good repair, but you need not leave it in better condition than a schedule of condition shows. A surveyor will photograph the unit on 9 October, at your cost, and the photographs will be attached to the lease.

Use

Baking and retail sales, and now a café for customers to eat and drink on the premises. You must obtain any planning permission the café needs, at your own cost.

Rent deposit

£13,500, which is a quarter’s rent with VAT. The landlord holds it for three years and then returns it, if you have paid the rent on time throughout.

Service charge

8.5% of the estate’s costs. On the current budget that is about £2,560 a year.

Plan

The bin store moves to the east side of the unit, away from the loading bay, on a revised plan.

The landlord has also agreed our other changes: interest on late rent starts after fourteen days, and the landlord’s costs of an application for consent are limited to its reasonable and proper costs, and only where it grants consent.

The deposit and the schedule of condition are the landlord’s price for the rent and break you wanted, and I think both are fair. The break is the more valuable term: it lets you leave after five years if the business has outgrown the unit.

Next steps. Please let me know that you approve these terms. We shall then prepare the lease for signing, and Chris Doyle will send it to you. It must be signed as a deed by a director, with a witness, before completion. The landlord’s solicitors are aiming for completion on 30 October 2026, and you will need to pay the deposit by then. Please tell me if that date does not suit you.
