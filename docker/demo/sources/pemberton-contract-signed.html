CONTRACT

for the sale of 14 Orchard Rise, Marlbridge WX4 3QT

Buyers’ part

Dated 23 October 2026

Parties

(1)    MRS DOROTHY LYLE, of 14 Orchard Rise, Marlbridge WX4 3QT (the Seller); and

(2)    MR OLIVER PEMBERTON and MS GRACE PEMBERTON, of Flat 3, 18 Bellfield Road, Marlbridge WX2 8LN (the Buyers).

The Seller’s solicitors are Tasker & Wynn (Neil Tasker, 01632 960718). The Buyers’ solicitors are Ashcombe Reeve LLP (Helen Ashcombe, 01632 960211).

The Property

14 Orchard Rise, Marlbridge WX4 3QT, a freehold house with its garden (the Property).

Terms

1.    Sale. The Seller sells and the Buyers buy the Property, freehold, with vacant possession on completion, for £385,000 (the Price).

2.    Deposit. On the date of this contract the Buyers shall pay £38,500, which is ten per cent of the Price, to Tasker & Wynn, who shall hold it as stakeholder until completion. On completion it is released to the Seller and counts as part of the Price. If this contract ends through no fault of the Buyers, it shall be returned to them at once.

3.    Completion. Completion shall take place on Friday 6 November 2026. By 2pm that day the Buyers shall pay the balance of the Price, £346,500, in cleared funds to Tasker & Wynn. Money that arrives after 2pm counts as received on the next working day, and for each day of delay the Buyers shall pay the Seller interest on the balance at 4 per cent a year.

4.    Possession. On completion the Seller shall give the Buyers the keys and vacant possession of the Property, empty of her belongings and of rubbish.

5.    Insurance. The Property is at the Buyers’ risk from the date of this contract. The Buyers shall insure it from that date for its full rebuilding cost. The Seller need not keep it insured after that date.

6.    What the Property is sold with and subject to. The Property is sold with the benefit of, and subject to:

(a)    a right of way over the drive that it shares with No. 16 Orchard Rise, which the owner of No. 16 also has over the part of the drive that belongs to the Property; and

(b)    covenants not to use the Property for business and not to keep caravans on it.

The Buyers have been told of these and buy subject to them.

7.    Fixtures and contents. The Price includes the fixtures and fittings of the house, among them its fitted kitchen and light fittings, and the shed in the garden. The Seller shall take her furniture and curtains away before completion.

8.    The Seller’s promises. The Seller promises that she owns the Property freehold, that any mortgage on it will be repaid on completion, and that she has told the Buyers’ solicitors of every dispute about the Property that she knows of.

9.    Delay. If either party does not complete on the date in clause 3, the other may give written notice allowing ten working days to complete. If the party who received the notice has still not completed when that time ends, the other may end this contract. The Seller may then keep the deposit, if it was the Buyers who did not complete.

10.    Whole agreement. This contract is the whole agreement between the Seller and the Buyers about the sale. The Buyers have relied only on what this contract says and on the written replies given to their solicitors’ enquiries.

11.    Law. English law governs this contract and any dispute about it, and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction.

Signed by the Buyers

This is the Buyers’ part of the contract. The Seller signs the other part, in the same terms. The two parts are exchanged on the date at the head of this contract, and the contract is made when they are.
