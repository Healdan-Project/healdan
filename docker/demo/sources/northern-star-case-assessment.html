Matter

Saltmarsh Shipping Ltd – Charter Dispute, MV Northern Star – prospects and settlement

1.   Where we are. Kingsholm claims USD 184,500.00 (10.25 days at USD 18,000.00). Our figure is USD 96,300.00 (5.35 days). The gap is USD 88,200.00, or 4.9 days. Our letter of 23 September offers USD 96,300.00 in full and final settlement, open until 7 October.

2.   When laytime started: strong. Clause 9.2 starts laytime at 08:00 on the working day after notice of readiness. Notice was given at 10:24 on Monday 3 August, so laytime began at 08:00 on Tuesday 4 August. The wording is plain and I expect the owners to struggle with it. That point, with the Sunday exception in clause 9.3, takes 1.9 days (USD 34,200.00) off their figure.

3.   The weather exception: good, not certain. Clause 9.4 stops the clock while weather stops discharge. The swell at the berth and the closing of the pilot station on 7 to 10 August (harbour notices 41/26 and 42/26) are well recorded. But the owners will say the closure made no difference to a vessel already at the berth, and that discharge could have gone on. I put our chances on this point at about 65%. It is worth 3 days (USD 54,000.00).
