Minutes of a meeting of the board of directors

Held by video call at 10:30 on 23 October 2026

Present

Ines Kowalczyk (chair); Tobias Wren

In attendance

Helen Ashcombe, Ashcombe Reeve LLP, the Company’s solicitors

1.   Quorum and interests. The chair noted that both directors were present and that the meeting was quorate. Each director declared that he or she is interested in the subscription agreement as a Founder who gives warranties under it. The board noted that the articles of association now in force allow a director to vote on a matter in which he or she is interested, once the interest is declared.

2.   The subscription agreement. It was resolved to approve the subscription agreement in the agreed form of 15 October 2026 between the Company, the Founders, Merrowdown Ventures LLP and Pellridge Capital Partners LP, and the Disclosure Letter, and that Ines Kowalczyk sign them for the Company.

3.   New articles. The board noted that the shareholders had that morning passed a written resolution, as a special resolution, that adopts new articles of association, and that the shareholders’ rights of pre-emption on the allotment below had been waived.

4.   Allotment. It was resolved to allot 2,500,000 Series A preferred shares at £1.60 each, for £4,000,000 in all, as follows: 1,875,000 shares to Merrowdown Ventures LLP for £3,000,000 and 625,000 shares to Pellridge Capital Partners LP for £1,000,000. The allotment is to take effect when the Company has received the money in cleared funds in its bank account.

5.   A new director. It was resolved to appoint Felix Amberley a director of the Company from completion. The board noted his signed consent to act.

6.   Certificates and filings. It was resolved to issue share certificates to Merrowdown Ventures LLP and Pellridge Capital Partners LP, to bring the Company’s registers up to date, and to file at the companies registry the return of allotment, the new articles and the notice of Felix Amberley’s appointment, within the time the law allows.

7.   Close. There was no other business, and the chair closed the meeting at 10:45.

Signed as a correct record:
