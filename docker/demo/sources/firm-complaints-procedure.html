Complaints Procedure

For clients of Ashcombe Reeve LLP – version October 2026 – owner Helen Ashcombe, Partner – next review October 2027

Our commitment

We aim to give every client a service they are happy with. If you are not, we want to hear about it, so that we can put it right. We will treat every complaint seriously and fairly, and use what we learn to do better.

How to complain

You can tell the person who is handling your matter. If you would rather not, or they cannot settle it, please complain to Helen Ashcombe, the partner who deals with complaints. You can write to her at Ashcombe House, 9 Quay Street, Marlbridge WX1 4AB, email helen.ashcombe@ashcombe-reeve.example, or ring her on 01632 960211. Tell us who you are, which matter it is about, what has gone wrong and what you would like us to do. If you need help to make a complaint, tell us and we will arrange it.

What we will do

1.   We will acknowledge your complaint in writing within three working days.

2.   Someone who has not been involved in your matter will investigate it. If your complaint is about Helen, Tom Reeve will deal with it.

3.   We will read your file and speak to the people involved, and we will offer to talk to you by telephone or video, or at our office.

4.   We will give you our answer within 14 days of receiving your complaint. If we need longer, we will say why and what we are doing, and we will send you our final response within eight weeks at the most.

Complaints about our bills

If you are unhappy with a bill, tell us, and we will review it with you. Please pay any part of it that you do not dispute. You may also have a right to ask a court to assess a bill; if you tell us you are considering that, we will explain how it works and any time limit.

No charge

We never charge for dealing with a complaint.

If you are still unhappy

If you are not content with our final response, or we have not given you one within eight weeks, you can ask the independent ombudsman for legal services to look at your complaint. There are time limits for doing so. We will give you the ombudsman’s contact details, and tell you the time limits, when we send you our final response, and at any time you ask. Using the ombudsman does not stop you taking any other action the law allows.
