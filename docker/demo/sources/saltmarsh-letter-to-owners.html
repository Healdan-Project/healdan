Kingsholm Tonnage Ltd and Saltmarsh Shipping Ltd – MV Northern Star – laytime and demurrage at Hartmouth

We act for Saltmarsh Shipping Ltd, the charterers of the MV Northern Star under a voyage charterparty dated 12 March 2026. We refer to your letter of 15 September 2026, which claims demurrage of USD 184,500.00 for the discharge at Hartmouth.

Our client does not accept that figure.
