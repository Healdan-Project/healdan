Subject

File retention from 1 November 2026

1.   Where closed files live. From 1 November closed files are kept in Healdan, not on paper. When a matter closes, its paper file is scanned into the matter, the fee earner checks the scan is complete and readable, and only then is the paper shredded.

2.   How long we keep them. Most files are kept for 7 years after closing. Conveyancing files (house purchases, sales and leases) are kept for 15 years. A file for a child is kept until the child is 25.

3.   Wills and deeds. Original wills and deeds stay in the strongroom until the client collects them. We keep a scanned copy for good.

4.   Disposal. Each January Sam Okafor reviews which files have reached the end of their time and sends the partners a list. Nothing is deleted until a partner has approved the list.

If you are unsure how long a file should be kept, ask me or Sam before you close it.
