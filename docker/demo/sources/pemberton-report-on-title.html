Dear Oliver and Grace

Your purchase of 14 Orchard Rise, Marlbridge WX4 3QT from Mrs Dorothy Lyle for £385,000 – report on title

We have now looked at the seller’s title and received the results of the searches. This letter tells you what we found and what we need from you. Please read it carefully and call Jamie Clarke or me if anything is unclear.

The title

The house is freehold and its title is registered, so you will own it outright. Two things affect it. First, the drive in front of it is shared with No. 16: No. 14 has a right of way over the drive, and the owner of No. 16 has the same right over the part that belongs to you, so neither of you may block it. Second, the title is subject to two old covenants: not to use the property for business, and not to keep caravans on it. Neither stops you living there as a family. If you plan to run any kind of business from the house, please tell us before you commit.

The searches

The local authority, drainage and water, and environmental searches are all clear. They show that the road is maintained by the council, that the house is connected to the public sewer and the mains water supply, and that nothing unusual affects the land.

Your mortgage

Eastwick and District Building Society has offered you £300,000. Its conditions are that the house is insured from exchange of contracts for its full rebuilding cost, that you live in it as your home and do not let it without the Society’s written consent, and that we confirm to it that the title is good. We shall also ask the Society to send the money to us a few days before completion.

The money you need

On exchange you pay a deposit of 10% of the price, which is £38,500. Stamp duty land tax is £4,250. You qualify for first-time buyers’ relief, because you rent your flat and neither of you has owned a home: nothing is due on the first £300,000 of the price, and 5% on the other £85,000. We shall pay the tax for you and register your ownership. We shall send you a completion statement that shows everything you must pay, and when.

Insurance

From the moment contracts are exchanged the house is at your risk, so you must insure it from then. The Society requires this too. Please send us the details of the policy before exchange.

Timetable

If you approve this report, we propose to exchange contracts on Friday 23 October 2026 and to complete on Friday 6 November 2026. Before exchange we shall need you both to sign the contract and your mortgage deed. Please tell us at once if either date does not suit you.
