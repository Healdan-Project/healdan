Landlord

Tenant

Premises

Unit 4, Weir Lane Trading Estate, Marlbridge WX2 6DN, with its yard, the loading bay in front of it, and car parking spaces 7 and 8, as shown on the landlord’s lease plan.

Term

Ten years from 1 November 2026.

Rent

£48,000 a year plus VAT, payable quarterly in advance.

At the end of the fifth year, the rent rises by the percentage rise in the consumer prices index over those five years.

Full repairing: the tenant keeps the premises in good repair throughout the term.

A bakery, with retail sales of its products to the public from the unit.

Service charge

The tenant pays a fair share of the costs of running the estate: its road, lighting, landscaping, security and management. The share is now 8.5%.

Insurance

The landlord insures the premises. The tenant pays its share of the premium.

Break

None.
