What's new compared with the Classic Interface

  • Access is Read Only, Read/Write or Full Access, as in iManage, with Show Every Permission for the detail.
  • New Matter shows what it will create, and catches a permission problem before you press Create.
  • Folder permissions and the folder tree sit side by side.

What I'd like from you

  • Pick A or B. I'd go with B: a workspace needs the room, and Workspace Templates can use the same page.
  • Tell me what's wrong, in the conversation.
A

Panel beside the list

Quick to skim matters, but too narrow to change a workspace.

B

A page for each matter

Room for folders and permissions together, one click further from the list.
