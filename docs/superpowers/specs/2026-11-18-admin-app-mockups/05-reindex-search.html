The rule for the whole app

  • Anything that can grow long is a search: matters, clients and people, in forms and in the filters above lists.
  • So New Matter's Client and Owner become searches too, and so do the Client and Owner filters on the Matters list.
  • Short lists stay plain menus: Stores and workspace templates.

Shortcuts

  • Reindex on a matter's page or a folder opens this with that item already chosen.
  • The search matches a client's name too, so typing a client finds all its matters.
