What it shows

  • The subject, sender, To and Cc, and both dates, read from the filed message.
  • Attachments as chips. Clicking one previews it if it's a PDF or a picture, or downloads it. Save to Matter files it as a document of its own in this email's folder, with the email's sent properties and an attachment link, as filing from Outlook does (M5). One already filed shows its #.
  • The email's own text. Its HTML is cleaned and shown in a sandboxed frame, so no script in a message ever runs.
  • Open opens the .eml or .msg in your mail app, as double-click does.

Pictures from the internet

  • Many emails load pictures from the sender's server, and some are trackers that tell the sender each time the email is opened, and from where.
  • I propose blocking them by default, as Outlook and Apple Mail can, with Show Pictures for that viewing only. Pictures sent inside the message always show.
