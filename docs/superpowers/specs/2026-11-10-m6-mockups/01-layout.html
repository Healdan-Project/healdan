What's settled, drawn as the roadmap has it

  • Scope picker joined to the search box; / jumps to it.
  • Left: the four search scopes, Recent Matters, the My Matters tree (search folders inside it), My Favorites.
  • Breadcrumb as client › client.matter – name › folder.
  • Title, Doc #, Version, Edit Date (plus Author) in the list; double-click opens in the app.
  • Right: the selected item's details, or the filters.

What I've had to guess

  • The four scopes on the left. I've read them as: each opens that scope, starting from your recent ones, and the search box then searches within it.
  • Details and Filters as two tabs you switch between.
  • "Helper" top right: whether Open in App will work on this computer.
  • A drop zone under the list, though the whole list takes drops.
  • Actions on a toolbar above the list as well as in the panel.

How should the right panel switch between details and filters?

A

Two tabs, Details and Filters

You choose. Selecting a document doesn't hide the filters you're using unless you're on Details.

B

It follows the selection

Filters while nothing is selected, Details once something is. Fewer clicks, but the panel changes under you as you click about.

C

Filters above the list, details on the right

Filters as a row of drop-downs over the list (Date, Type, Author…), so both show at once; the panel is only ever details.
