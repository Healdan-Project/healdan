Proposed

  • Suggestions as you type: matching matters first, then a few documents by title. Enter runs the full search in the chosen scope, and the arrow keys pick a suggestion.
  • #10388 then Enter shows results with just that document, already selected with its details showing, ready to act on (principle 1). Location takes you to its folder. #10388.2 selects version 2 in Versions. A number nobody can read says "No document # 10388 that you can see", the same words whether it exists or not.
  • With the Matters scope, a matter number such as 1001.1 then Enter opens the matter.
  • In results, the Matter column shows the matter number with the client muted, and the matter's name on hover. Location in the details takes you to the folder.
  • Results come by relevance, and any column header sorts them instead. Filters add Client and Matter here.

Behind the dim list in state 1

  • Clicking Documents in the left pane shows Recent Documents: yours, newest first. That's my reading of the four scopes on the left. Emails shows your recent emails, and Folders your recent folders.
  • Clients is the odd one out. M1 has no client list, so for now I'd have it show the clients of your recent matters and My Matters.

What should the four scopes in the left pane do?

A

Each opens its own recent items

Documents: Recent Documents. Emails: recent emails. Folders: recent folders. Clients: the clients you work with. Each also sets the scope picker, so the next search runs in that scope.

B

They only set the scope

A click picks that scope and puts the cursor in the search box. Recent Documents moves to the start page instead.

C

Leave them out

The scope picker already does this, so the left pane starts at Recent Matters, with a single Recent Documents entry above it.
