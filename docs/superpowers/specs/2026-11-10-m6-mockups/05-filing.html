Either way

  • Where you can drop: anywhere on a folder's list, or on a folder in the My Matters tree, which lights up. A matter's top level and search folders refuse, with "Choose a folder inside the matter" (the filing rule).
  • Emails (.eml, .msg) take their subject as the title and fill From, To, CC, Sent and Received themselves. One already in the matter is never filed twice, and you're shown its #.
  • Dropping one file on a document row asks "Save as Version 4 of # 10431, or File as a New Document?", as iManage does.
  • The defaults are the folder's own (its Class, if it sets one), and you as Author and Typist. Client and Matter always come from the matter.
  • The uploads keep going if you move to another folder. Leaving the page warns you while any are still uploading.

The difference

  • A asks every time, like the helper's Save to Healdan. You get it right first time, at the cost of a click per drop.
  • B files straight away with the defaults, and new rows flash green in the list. You fix what needs fixing afterwards, with Edit in the tray or the details panel.
  • C is B, but the form appears whenever a required field has no default, and holding ⌥ while dropping asks for it anyway.

How should a drop file its documents?

A

A form first

The form appears on every drop, prefilled. Nothing is filed until you press File.

B

File at once

Filed with the defaults straight away. The tray shows each document's # and lets you edit it.

C

At once, unless something's missing

B, but the form appears when a required field has no default, or when you hold ⌥ while dropping.
