A · Reading pane

  • Preview (the toolbar, the details panel or the Space key) swaps the details region for a wide preview. It stays open while you click or arrow down the list, like Outlook's reading pane.
  • The list keeps only Title, Doc # and Edit Date while it's narrow.
  • Close it, and the region goes back to details.
  • Good for working through a folder. It takes half the screen.

B · Quick Look

  • Space opens a large preview over everything, as Finder's Quick Look does. Space or Esc closes it.
  • ‹ › and the arrow keys move through the list behind it, so you can still flick through a folder.
  • The list and the panels keep their layout; nothing reflows.
  • Good for "is this the right document?", the use M1.5 was built for.

Which preview fits how you work?

A

Reading pane

A preview that stays docked beside the list.

B

Quick Look

A large preview over the list, opened and closed with Space.

C

Both

Space opens Quick Look, and a button in it docks the preview as a reading pane, which stays docked until you close it.
