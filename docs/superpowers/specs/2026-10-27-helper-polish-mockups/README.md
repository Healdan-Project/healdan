# Helper and extension polish mockups

The mockups the owner reviewed, section by section, in the polish design session on 2026-10-27 ([spec](../2026-10-27-helper-ui-polish-design.md)). They were drawn inline in the conversation. Each file here is that drawing, with a short head added so it opens in a browser on its own: it loads Tabler's icon webfont from jsDelivr, and stands in for the few colours the conversation's frame supplied.

They're illustrations. Where a mockup and the spec differ, the spec wins:

- `foundations.html`: the palette, type, controls and icons, in light and dark. The spec darkens the dark accent fill to `#1A74C4` so that white text on it passes AA.
- `window-shells.html`: the form (Sign In), alert (Checked Out on Another Computer) and browser (Open's unified title bar) shells. The search placeholder's wording is the spec's (§6.2).
- `open-window.html`: Open from Healdan. Its details pane is the one before the owner asked for tabs, and the list drops the Matter column and shortens the holder text only to fit. `open-details-tabs.html` replaces the pane.
- `open-details-tabs.html`: the details pane's header and its Properties and Versions tabs (owner).
- `settings-and-about.html`: Settings as grouped rows, and About. About's copyright and credit lines are placeholders: the spec keeps today's.
- `menubar-and-libreoffice.html`: the menu bar icon, the LibreOffice toolbar and an infobar. Its Save and Open icons were replaced by the owner's choice in the next file.
- `libreoffice-open-save-icons.html`: Save to Healdan as a floppy disk and Open from Healdan as a folder. The owner chose B, grey with the shield.
