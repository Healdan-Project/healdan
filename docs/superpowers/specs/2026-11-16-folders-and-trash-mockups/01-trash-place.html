Which way in?

A

Its own place at the foot of the left pane

Where the Classic Interface and most desktop apps keep it, and always in view. It takes a row at the bottom of the pane, apart from the lists above.

B

Under Recent, as Recently Deleted

Beside the other things you've touched lately, as Apple's Photos and Notes put it. Nothing new in the pane's shape, but "Trash" isn't the word on it, and the Classic Interface and the User Guide call it the Trash.

Either way (my proposals)

  • A deleted folder is one row, with how many items it holds. What was deleted inside it comes back with it, so its contents aren't listed separately. Documents you deleted on their own get rows of their own.
  • Deleted From is the folder each item came from, by matter number. Restore puts it back there; only if that place has gone, or won't take it, does Healdan ask where to.
  • Remove… and Empty Trash… each confirm first. You won't be able to restore what you remove; an administrator can, for 30 days. The admin's view and the confirmations are the next screen.
  • Keyboard and menus as the other lists: Delete on a row is Remove…, Restore on its right-click menu, Enter restores.
