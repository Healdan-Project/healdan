Behind the screens (my proposals)

  • Greyed out where it can't be done, as Rename is: Move To… and Delete read new canMove and canDelete on each folder, from the folder itself and M1's rules. The check of everything inside is too costly to make for every row, so it runs when you ask, and a refusal like the ones above answers it.
  • The Delete key on a folder's row, or in the tree, deletes it; Move To… is the keyboard's way to move, as for documents.
  • Folders and documents selected together move or delete together, each its own call, and what's refused is said per item while the rest is done, as for documents now. That needs folder rows to be selectable, which they aren't today.
  • A matter's own folder never shows Move To… or Delete here: moving a matter between Stores and deleting a matter are the Admin app's.
