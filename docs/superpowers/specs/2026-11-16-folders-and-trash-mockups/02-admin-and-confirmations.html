Behind the screens (my proposals)

  • Everyone's is every live item in a Trash, with a Deleted By column, and Restore and Remove… as on Mine. It's for admins only in the app. The Classic Interface's Deleted Documents and Deleted Folders reports keep their own menu permission.
  • Removed has Restore only. Nothing in the app destroys early: destruction is the nightly job's, after the 30 days (a setting in Healdan Settings).
  • Destroyed counts down to the job's night; under a week it's shown in the caution colour.
  • Restoring from Removed works as from the Trash: back where it was deleted from, or Healdan asks where.
  • The record of who deleted, removed, restored and destroyed each item is kept in Healdan's own table, and outlives the item. Until it's destroyed, Removed's columns show it. Once it's destroyed, nothing in this phase shows it: a screen for it waits for the Admin app, unless you want one now.
